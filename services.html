Ways I can help

A useful starting point, not a giant engagement.

These are examples of focused projects. We’ll confirm the need and scope before any work starts.

01

Get your business details in order

Make services, hours, contact details, and common questions consistent across the pages you control. I can help write a clear business profile and add structured data where it fits, then check what’s actually published.

Useful when customers keep finding conflicting or incomplete information online.

02

Build a site you can keep current

A focused business website with a simple way to request changes by text or Telegram. You review edits before they go live, with a record of what changed and a way to roll back.

Useful when hours, events, menus, or services go stale because updates take too long.

03

Find the workflow worth fixing

We’ll look at a repeated task, the tools involved, and where time or information gets lost. You’ll get a short, prioritized plan before deciding whether to build anything.

A good first step when you know the work is clunky but aren’t sure why.

04

Automate one repeatable task

Connect a defined workflow across the tools you already use, then test common cases, add error alerts, and document how it works. The goal is fewer manual steps and a process you can still understand.

Best for a stable task you can show me with real examples.

05

Launch ads with clear measurement

Set up a focused campaign around a real offer, a working destination, and a result you can measure. That can include creative variants, conversion checks, and a first-month readout.

Ad spend is separate. We’ll confirm your goal and budget first.

06

Reach a relevant newsletter audience

Ask about a clearly labeled sponsorship in Vermont South for a local business or event, or in Spotter for a product relevant to its HubSpot-focused readers.

Placement, timing, audience information, and pricing are confirmed before booking.

How we start

Show me the messy version.

You don’t need to know which service fits. Tell me what keeps breaking, what you want to change, or what you’re hoping to promote. I’ll ask a few questions, suggest a sensible first step, and put scope and cost in writing if it’s a fit.
